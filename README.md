## Vein-Finder
A low cost homemade device to help people find veins in their arms, legs, etc.

<img width="1600" height="1200" alt="HC SKETCH (7)" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/5081df08-86b4-40d5-b349-396c4262c2e9" />

## Features
1. Can detect veins with just a 18650 Li Ion Battery
2. Vein Finder can be used in emergency situations where you need to inject something inside someone's blood vessel
3. Low cost to build (Costs less than $5 USD)
4. Easy to assemble (Divided into only 4 parts which makes it a simple and friendly beginner project)

## Background
I thought of this project when I was brainstorming with my friend (she was going to go to med school) and she casually mention on how frustrating it is to find the blood vessels in people's arms. I thought of it for awhile and went to Claude to do some research for a tool or device to help doctors or med students find blood vessels in parts of the body easily. There was a company that made similar but better products than mine but the price is overwhelmingly expensive and it is mostly used in hospitals or medical insitutions. Their vein finder costs around $4000 (brand new) and it seems like its not the best option for everyone. The vein finder that I made does its job well and still being affordable to anyone. It can be put in emergency kits or even just as a tool at home incase anything happens.

## How it Works
It works by using red LED to show the veins in the arms. The LED works by using a specific wavelength (625nm) to penetrate skin, tissues, etc. The device is very cost efficient which makes it practical for accessories in medical emergency kits. PLA is used to make the casing and it uses less than 50 grams of filament. The Vein finder also is a low cost device with less than $5 to make the whole thing that makes it practical for every family or medical students from around the world.

## How to use it
1. Turn on the device (there should be a switch)
2. Aim the head to your arm or the targeted area
3. You should be able to see veins from the hole gap on the head
